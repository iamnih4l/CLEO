[h2] Scientifically Defensible MODIS–VIIRS Harmonization Methodology
[p] Transparent 8-stage pipeline architecture, mathematical formulations, and sensor limitations.
[div] STAGE 1 & 2
[h3] Ingestion & Source ID

[a] Standard Archive

[div] STAGE 3 & 4
[h3] Schema & Quality Control
[p] Normalizes coordinates, UTC timestamps, and thermal channels (`brightness`/`bright_t31` vs `bright_ti4`/`bright_ti5`). Maps MODIS numeric confidence (`0–100%`) and VIIRS categorical confidence (`l`/`n`/`h`) into `LOW`, `NOMINAL`, `HIGH` without equating raw scales. Preserves missing values as `null`.
[div] STAGE 5 & 6
[h3] Spherical Space-Time Grid
[p] Projects detections onto a configurable lat-lon grid (`0.10°`, `0.25°`, `0.50°`) using exact spherical zone area $A = R_\oplus^2 \Delta\lambda |\sin\phi_2 - \sin\phi_1|$ to compute
[strong] Unique Active Cell-Days
[p] and density ($\text{detections}/1,000\text{ km}^2$) in UTC.
[div] STAGE 7 & 8
[h3] Cross-Sensor & MAD Anomalies
[p] Performs haversine co-location matching (`1–5 km`) during overlap periods (`2012–2026`) and computes non-parametric Median Absolute Deviation ($\text{MAD}$) robust z-scores ($z_{\text{rob}}$) when historical baseline length $N \ge 5$.
