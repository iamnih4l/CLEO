# Brag Plan: CLEO — Earth-Observation Fire Intelligence

## What is this app?
CLEO (Cross-Sensor Earth Observation & Unified Fire Intelligence) is an Earth-observation analytics platform developed for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge that harmonizes 26 years of fragmented NASA satellite fire records across MODIS Collection 6.1 (1 km) and VIIRS Collection 2 (375 m) into a sensor-aware spatial intelligence interface with equal-area grid cells (GFED4 convention), non-parametric MAD anomaly detection, and a burning activity calendar.

## The angle
From fragmented satellite observations to a unified view of Earth's fire activity. Not an ordinary startup launch, but a cinematic, scientifically rigorous Earth-observation film that communicates the critical sensor resolution gap between MODIS and VIIRS, demonstrates the real working application, and presents authentic historical burning intelligence without false trends.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cinematic rotating Earth in the dark void of space, surrounded by atmospheric nebula glows, live orbital trajectories, and radiant thermal plume markers erupting across continents. "EARTH IS ALWAYS CHANGING. AND FIRE LEAVES A RECORD."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Sensor Disparity (Scene 2)**: Side-by-side comparison of MODIS (1 km diamond footprint, coarse) vs VIIRS (375 m ember circles, 7x pixel density), visually exposing the scientific challenge of comparability across 26 years.
- **Introducing CLEO (Scene 3)**: Tactical aerospace HUD camera sweep into the global active fire map, live UTC mission clock, and 0.25° equal-area spatial grid cells.
- **The Intelligence in Action (Scene 4)**: Interactive Area of Interest (AOI) selection triggering the Burning Activity Calendar heatmap matrix and the non-parametric Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) robust z-score anomaly engine.

## Outro / punchline
A sweeping wide view of Earth laced with illuminated fire observation networks. CLEO wordmark settles with authoritative scientific attribution: "UNDERSTANDING EARTH'S BURNING PATTERNS. DATA: NASA FIRMS | MODIS | VIIRS."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Global thermal telemetry with live satellite orbits (Terra, Aqua, Suomi-NPP, NOAA-20).
2. **Key Action**: AOI bounding box filter (South Asia / Mediterranean / Australia) harmonizing raw detections into 0.25° Active Cell-Days.
3. **Result**: The Burning Activity Calendar and robust MAD anomaly engine revealing seasonal fire distributions and anomalous periods.
