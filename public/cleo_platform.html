Scientifically Defensible MODIS–VIIRS Harmonization Methodology

Transparent 8-stage pipeline architecture, mathematical formulations, and sensor limitations.

STAGE 1 & 2

Ingestion & Source ID

Ingests records from NASA FIRMS Area API and Standard Archive. Identifies platform (`Terra`, `Aqua`, `Suomi-NPP`, `NOAA-20`, `NOAA-21`), collection (`6.1` vs `2.0`), and processing stream (`SP` vs `NRT`).

STAGE 3 & 4

Schema & Quality Control

Normalizes coordinates, UTC timestamps, and thermal channels (`brightness`/`bright_t31` vs `bright_ti4`/`bright_ti5`). Maps MODIS numeric confidence (`0–100%`) and VIIRS categorical confidence (`l`/`n`/`h`) into `LOW`, `NOMINAL`, `HIGH` without equating raw scales. Preserves missing values as `null`.

STAGE 5 & 6

Spherical Space-Time Grid

Projects detections onto a configurable lat-lon grid (`0.10°`, `0.25°`, `0.50°`) using exact spherical zone area $A = R_\oplus^2 \Delta\lambda |\sin\phi_2 - \sin\phi_1|$ to compute Unique Active Cell-Days and density ($\text{detections}/1,000\text{ km}^2$) in UTC.

STAGE 7 & 8

Cross-Sensor & MAD Anomalies

Performs haversine co-location matching (`1–5 km`) during overlap periods (`2012–2026`) and computes non-parametric Median Absolute Deviation ($\text{MAD}$) robust z-scores ($z_{\text{rob}}$) when historical baseline length $N \ge 5$.

Critical Scientific Limitations & Disclaimers

  • Aggregation vs. Physical Calibration: CLEO performs common space-time aggregation (`Active Cell-Days`) and sensor-stratified comparison. It does not invent physical cross-sensor regression multipliers because the VIIRS/MODIS ratio varies by biome and fire size (Majumdar 2025).
  • Active Hotspots ≠ Burned Area: Detections represent thermal anomalies at satellite overpass time (`1 km` or `375 m` pixel centers), not total burned perimeters (`MCD64A1`). Cloud cover, smoke, and canopy mask active fires (NASA FIRMS FAQ).
  • Suomi-NPP Cessation (Nov 2, 2026): Delivery of Suomi-NPP VIIRS science data ended on November 2, 2026 at 15:40 UTC (NASA LANCE Notice). Ongoing VIIRS continuity is provided by NOAA-20 (`JPSS-1`) and NOAA-21 (`JPSS-2`).
  • Monitoring Support Only: Statistical anomalies are historical analytical indicators, not confirmed emergency dispatches or future wildfire predictions.
